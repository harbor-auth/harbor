Create your Harbor account

Harbor signs you into the apps you choose without becoming a place that profiles you.

  • We don't build a profile of you or sell your data.
  • Each app you connect gets its own separate identifier — never a shared account key those apps can compare notes with.
  • You only ever share the specific details an app asks for, and only after you consent.
  • Every connection is visible in your dashboard, where you can review or revoke it at any time.
